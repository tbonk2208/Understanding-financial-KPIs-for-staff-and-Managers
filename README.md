# Understanding Financial KPIs for Staff and Managers

A self-study tutorial that explains financial key performance indicators and translates each one into four role tracks:

- Operations & supply chain
- Sales & marketing
- R&D & engineering
- Managers with budget or P&L responsibility

**Independent learning material. No real company is named or described. Not investment advice.**

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in any browser (download the file, or clone the repository and double-click it). No server, build step or internet connection is needed. Pick a role at the top; progress is remembered in the browser.

## Content

| Module | Topic | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Method and the one-minute picture | Example group |
| 1 to 10 | Shared core: three statements, growth, margins, EBITDA, segment EBITDA, working capital and cash, leverage, EPS, return on capital, reading a quarterly report | Example group |
| 11 | The example plant: a consistent invented data set for plant-level KPIs | Example plant |
| 12 | Role track, written separately for each of the four roles: line of sight to the group result, role KPIs, worked cases, calculator, exercises with solutions, self-test | Example plant |
| 13 | Decision lab: pick one of 38 everyday decisions, filtered by role, and see which indicators move, in which direction, why, and what the trade-off is | Qualitative |
| 14 | Reading the signals: 23 cases of several figures read together, filtered by role; each answer shows per KPI what it suggests alone and what it contributes to the conclusion, marks the key clue, and lists what to check next and the decisions behind it | Illustrative |
| 15 | How the KPIs connect: an interactive map that follows the business in seven steps from the sale to the shareholder, in which any figure can be set to rise or fall to see what follows, and six labs (live return tree with CFROI, cash-cycle timeline, profit versus cash when stock is built, break-even chart, mix effect, ROCE versus CFROI as a machine ages) | Both, labelled |
| 16 | KPI catalogue: 76 indicators with formula, worked value, what the value means in practice (computed from the data), how to read it, which decisions move it and how each role influences it; searchable, filterable by topic and by role | Both, labelled |

Modules 2 to 9 each contain a "How to read it" part and a table of the decisions that move the figure. Module 12, the decision lab and the catalogue highlights change with the selected role.

## Data and limits

- The **example group** is invented: a fictional listed industrial company with two segments. Its figures are internally consistent and of a realistic order of magnitude, but describe no real company.
- The **example plant**, its customers and its development project are invented. Values are labelled "illustrative".
- All profit figures are reported figures. EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortisation; the tutorial uses no adjusted (non-GAAP) measures.
- Values labelled "own calculation", "own estimate" or "illustration" use simplified textbook formulas; company-specific definitions often differ.
- Internal KPI definitions, targets and incentive systems of any company are not covered.

## Changing the figures

1. Open `index.html` and find the `DATA` block at the top of the `<script>` section (example group). The example plant is in the `EX` block directly below it.
2. Replace the figures. Tables, charts, ratios and calculators recompute automatically.
3. Review the sentences that explain causes (for example the growth drivers in Module 2 and the worked example in Module 10) and the guidance table in Module 10, which are written by hand.
